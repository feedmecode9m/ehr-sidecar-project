## Part 8 — Contracts Without a Lawyer (minimum viable)

1. **Mutual NDA** — before sharing clinic workflows (Mutual is fair)  
2. **Statement of Work (SOW)** — scope, deliverables, timeline, payment  
3. **Independent contractor clause** — you control methods; not employee  
4. **IP assignment** — client owns deliverables **they paid for**; you retain pre-existing tools  
5. **W-9** — send before first payment  

**Free/low-cost sources:** Bonsai, AND.CO, HelloSign templates; or copy from [Docracy](https://www.docracy.com) independent contractor SOW.

**Optional edge:** General liability + E&O insurance (~$30–80/mo) — mention "insured contractor" on capability PDF when you have it.
