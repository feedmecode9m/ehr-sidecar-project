## Part 10 — Interview Answers (nonprofit + HealthTech)

**“Why healthcare?”**  
> I’ve seen compliance and burnout from the ops side — long ticket queues, staff juggling spreadsheets next to clinical systems. Engineering can remove clicks and protect data at the same time. The EHR Sidecar is my proof that I design for that reality.

**“Why a nonprofit model?”**  
> Enterprise vendors optimize for hospital budgets. FQHCs, free clinics, and community health programs need the same safety patterns — allergy visibility, sane workflows — without seven-figure contracts. Open demos, donation-funded maintenance, and FHIR-ready architecture is how we meet them in the ecosystem.

**“What’s your first product?”**  
> Phase 1: donation + campaign infrastructure with receipting and transparent fund routing. Phase 2: white-label clinical sidecar for clinics already on legacy EHRs. EHR Sidecar is the technical prototype for Phase 2.

**“How do you handle PHI?”**  
> Server-side aggregation, minimum necessary fields to the client, noteId-only AI requests, no PHI in URLs or logs, Zod on every API boundary — habits I keep even on mock data so they’re default in production.
